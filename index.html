THAILAND · AUTHENTIC FLAVORS

Tom Yum Goong

A classic Thai hot and sour soup made with shrimp, lemongrass, galangal, and fragrant lime leaves. Every spoonful is full of bold and refreshing flavor.

◷ 20 minutes ♨ Hot & Sour ✦ Thai Cuisine

“The key to a delicious Tom Yum Goong lies in the balance of flavors – sweet, sour, salty, and spicy.”

— Chef Somchai

Ingredients

What you need for this Thai classic.

  • Lemongrass2 stalks
  • Thai Basil1 cup
  • Kaffir Lime Leaves3 leaves
  • Shrimp500 g
  • Fish SauceTo taste
  • Lime JuiceTo taste
  • Chili PasteTo taste
  • Galangal and BrothAs needed

Note: Some quantities were not provided in the original recipe.

Preparation

Five simple steps to prepare your soup.

  1. 01
    Simmer the broth

    Simmer the broth for 10 minutes.

  2. 02
    Add the aromatics

    Add lemongrass, galangal, and kaffir lime leaves.

  3. 03
    Cook the shrimp

    Add shrimp and cook until pink and cooked through.

  4. 04
    Season the soup

    Add fish sauce, lime juice, and chili paste to taste.

  5. 05
    Garnish and serve

    Add Thai basil and serve while hot.

✦

A Little Cooking Tip

Adjust the lime juice, fish sauce, and chili paste to balance the sour, salty, and spicy flavors.

🍲

Ready to Serve

Enjoy your Tom Yum Goong with steamed rice or jasmine tea.
